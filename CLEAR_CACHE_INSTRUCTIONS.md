## ✅ Solution: Clear Your Browser Cache

### Method 1: Hard Refresh (Quick)
1. Open the profile page: `http://localhost:8000/frontend/templates/profile.html`
2. Press **Ctrl + Shift + R** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd + Shift + R** (Mac)
3. Or press **Ctrl + F5** (Windows/Linux)

### Method 2: Clear All Cache (Recommended)
#### Chrome:
1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Delete**
2. Select "Cached images and files"
3. Time range: "All time"
4. Click "Clear data"

#### Firefox:
1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Delete**
2. Check "Cache"
3. Time range: "Everything"
4. Click "Clear Now"

#### Edge:
1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + Delete**
2. Select "Cached images and files"
3. Time range: "All time"
4. Click "Clear now"

### Method 3: Open in Incognito/Private Mode
1. Press **Ctrl + Shift + N** (Chrome/Edge) or **Ctrl + Shift + P** (Firefox)
2. Navigate to your site
3. This will show the current version without cache
