Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers!

How does ImproveCommunication work?
After signing up, you can browse available users and start a audio call with anyone online. During your conversation, our AI analyzes your speech in real-time. After the call ends, you'll receive detailed feedback on your grammar, fluency, and communication skills.
Is there a time limit for calls?
Free users can talk for up to 5 minutes per call. You'll receive a warning at 4 minutes, and the call will automatically end at 5 minutes. Premium subscribers enjoy unlimited call duration. Note: Calls must be at least 1 minute long to receive feedback and count toward your statistics.
How accurate is the AI feedback?
Our AI uses advanced speech recognition and natural language processing to analyze your conversations. While it provides helpful insights, it's designed to complement, not replace, human learning. Use the feedback as a guide to identify areas for improvement.
Is ImproveCommunication free?
Yes! You can create an account and start practicing for free with a 5-minute call limit. We also offer premium subscriptions that provide unlimited call duration, advanced analytics, and priority support.
What if I don't speak during a call?
Both participants must actively speak during the call for it to be valid. If either person doesn't contribute meaningful conversation (at least a few sentences), the call won't count toward your statistics and no feedback will be generated. This ensures quality practice sessions.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We take privacy seriously. Your conversations are processed securely, and we never share your personal information with third parties. Call transcripts are used only to generate your feedback and are stored securely.
Who will I be matched with?
You can browse all available users and choose who you'd like to talk to. We recommend practicing with different people to experience various accents and communication styles. Everyone on the platform is here to learn and improve together!
How do I track my progress?
Your dashboard displays your overall AI score, total calls, average fluency score, and call history. You can also view the leaderboard to see how you compare with other learners. Remember, your initial stats will be zero until you complete your first valid call!
What equipment do I need?
All you need is a computer or mobile device with a working microphone and camera, plus a stable internet connection. We recommend using headphones to reduce echo during calls.
What if my question isn't answered here?
Feel free to reach out to our support team! You can contact us through the help section in your dashboard after logging in. We're here to help you succeed!
